Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Overview

radimoo helps people practice spoken English. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when using the app or website.

Information we collect

  • Account information, such as your sign-in provider, email address, and display name.
  • Practice preferences, including native language, English level, accent preference (US, UK, AU), daily goals, and selected situations.
  • Practice content, such as speech transcripts, AI feedback, scores, saved phrases, custom notes, personal example sentences, and progress history.
  • Practice voice audio: processed ephemerally in memory to transcribe and evaluate your speech. Raw practice recordings are never saved or stored on our servers.
  • Optional phrase dictation: when you tap a microphone beside a phrase or example field, your device's speech-recognition provider processes the audio and returns text to the app. Dictation audio is not sent to or stored by radimoo. Depending on your device, language, and settings, the provider may process it on the device or on its servers. Dictated text is handled like other text you enter and may be sent to radimoo when you choose to generate or save the phrase.
  • Device and notification information, including app version, device timezone, push notification tokens (FCM), notification preferences, and diagnostic logs (via Sentry.io).
  • Subscription details, such as active plan tier, renewal status, and app store transaction identifiers.

How we use information

  • Provide speaking practice, speech transcription, optional phrase dictation, feedback, phrase saving, and progress tracking.
  • Keep your profile and saved content available across devices when you sign in.
  • Deliver practice reminders and notifications based on your chosen settings.
  • Manage subscriptions, account security, support, and abuse prevention.
  • Diagnose app crashes, monitor performance, and improve reliability (using Sentry.io).

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate radimoo, such as authentication, hosting, analytics, payment, speech-to-text, AI-processing, push notification, and crash-reporting providers (including Sentry.io). Service providers engaged by radimoo process information to deliver the app under agreements with us. Optional phrase dictation uses the speech-recognition service supplied by your device platform, such as Apple Speech Recognition on iOS; that provider may process dictation audio under its own privacy terms.

Your choices & account deletion

You can choose not to create an account, though some features require sign-in. You can type phrases instead of using dictation, and you can deny or revoke microphone and speech-recognition permission in your device settings. You can also delete your account and all associated data directly inside the app settings at any time, which permanently purges your profile, transcripts, saved phrases, and history. You can also contact us to request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal information.

Contact

Questions or requests can be sent through radimoo support.

radimoo is in early development.
