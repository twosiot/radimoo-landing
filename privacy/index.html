Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Overview

radimoo helps people practice spoken English. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when using the app or website.

Information we collect

  • Account information, such as your sign-in provider, email address, and display name.
  • Practice preferences, including language, English level, accent preference, goals, and selected situations.
  • Practice content, such as transcripts, saved phrases, feedback, and progress history.
  • Audio you record during practice, when needed to transcribe or evaluate your speech.
  • Device and usage information, including anonymous device identifiers used for trial access, diagnostics, and basic app performance.

How we use information

  • Provide speaking practice, transcription, feedback, phrase saving, and progress tracking.
  • Keep your profile and saved content available across devices when you sign in.
  • Manage free access, subscriptions, account security, support, and abuse prevention.
  • Improve app reliability, user experience, and learning content.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate radimoo, such as authentication, hosting, analytics, payment, speech, and AI-processing providers. These providers are used to deliver the app and are expected to handle information according to their agreements with us.

Your choices

You can choose not to create an account, though some features may require sign-in. You can contact us to request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal information. Some information may be retained when required for security, legal, or operational reasons.

Contact

Questions or requests can be sent through radimoo support.

radimoo is in early development.
